pasankalla: enable travelmate by default

Travelmate now starts at boot and is no longer disabled while the WAN owns
the uplink: zz-pasankalla sets trm_enabled=1, and pasankalla only stops the
service and unplugs its STAs from network.trm_wwan so a STA lease cannot
outbid the WAN route (metric 100).

Uplink ownership is tracked in /tmp/pasankalla/tm because a running
travelmate removes its own rc.d link once nothing is left to scan, which
made the init's enabled flag unusable as state and re-enabled travelmate on
every poll. RADIO_CHANGED now restarts a running travelmate instead of
stopping it, and LED trigger/brightness files are only written when the
state changes.
